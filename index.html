The Silent Mirror

Book One of the Khalzam Trilogy

On Khalzam, two suns hang in the sky, and everything that makes life possible lies deep beneath the sand. Tariq hears it running: the stone around his neck trembles with the water veins under the desert. As long as he can follow that rhythm, he has a place among his people.

Then, at the Water Rite, something opens that will not close again, and he walks into the dunes that very night.

On the far side of the desert lies a city on black basalt, where water is counted and strangers are weighed. There someone bends over his stone and tells him what no one in the oasis ever said. Behind him, all the way from the caravan, walks someone who knows exactly what that stone is worth.

The prophecy has a second half. No one speaks it aloud.

And when the Mirror breaks, the river heart will sink into darkness, until the sacrifice of the dying restores the aquadrome.

About the Author

Andon Vey comes from a country that is flat and wet. Water there is something you pump away, guard, keep in check — never something you look for. Perhaps that is why he writes about thirst. One question will not let him go: what is left of a person once others begin to retell their story?
