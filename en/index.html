Book One

The Silent Mirror

Epic Fantasy · Coming Soon

On Khalzam, two suns hang in the sky, and everything that makes life possible lies deep beneath the sand. Tariq hears it running: the stone around his neck trembles with the water veins under the desert. As long as he can follow that rhythm, he has a place among his people.

Then, at the Water Rite, something opens that will not close again, and he walks into the dunes that very night. On the far side of the desert lies a city on black basalt, where water is counted and strangers are weighed. There, someone bends over his stone and tells him what no one in the oasis ever said.

The prophecy has a second half. No one speaks it aloud.

And when the Mirror breaks, the river heart will sink into darkness.

About the Author

A lens first, a story after

Andon Vey comes from a country that is flat and wet. Water there is something you pump away, guard, keep in check. Never something you look for. Perhaps that is why he writes about thirst.

He learned to look before he learned to tell. By day he works with systems that either hold or fail, with nothing in between. And always, there is the question that will not let him go: what is left of a person once others begin to retell their story?

He does not have the answer. He only needed a desert for it.
