The Khalzam Trilogy

Three volumes, one story

The titles read as a single sentence: a silent mirror, a labyrinth of runes, a mirror that falls silent. Written to be read in order. The Dutch edition appears first; the English edition follows.

Book One

The Silent Mirror

Coming Soon · The Dutch edition appears first

On Khalzam, everything that makes life possible lies deep beneath the sand. Tariq hears it running: the stone around his neck trembles with the water veins under the desert. As long as he can follow that rhythm, he has a place among his people.

Then, at the Water Rite, something opens that will not close again, and he walks into the dunes that very night. On the far side of the desert lies a city on black basalt, where a stone and a prophecy must both be answered.

And when the Mirror breaks, the river heart will sink into darkness.
