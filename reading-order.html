Reading Order

Where to Start

The Khalzam Trilogy is written to be read in order: each volume ends where the next begins. There is no other starting point, and no reading order for skipped strands — this is one story told in three parts.

I

The Silent Mirror

Begin here. A boy carries a stone, a city counts its water, and a prophecy has a second half that no one speaks aloud. The story of how a young man gives his gift away.

II

The Labyrinth of Runes

Ten months later. Tariq chooses his family over an expedition, and the search for the third stone points back into the deep. The story of the price of acting too soon.

III

The Mirror Falls Silent

Without a time gap. Tariq must restore the balance without resonance, and stand before a man who is not an enemy but a mirror. The story of letting go.
