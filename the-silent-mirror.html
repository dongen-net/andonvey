Book One of the Khalzam Trilogy

The Silent Mirror

Epic Fantasy · Coming Soon

On Khalzam, two suns hang in the sky, and everything that makes life possible lies deep beneath the sand. Tariq hears it running: the stone around his neck trembles with the water veins under the desert. As long as he can follow that rhythm, he has a place among his people.

Then, at the Water Rite, something opens that will not close again, and he walks into the dunes that very night.

On the far side of the desert lies a city on black basalt, where water is counted and strangers are weighed. There someone bends over his stone and tells him what no one in the oasis ever said: that the rune is older than the temple, and that he is a resonance bearer of a network that runs beneath the city.

Behind him, all the way from the caravan, walks someone who knows exactly what that stone is worth.

The prophecy has a second half. No one speaks it aloud.

And when the Mirror breaks, the river heart will sink into darkness, until the sacrifice of the dying restores the aquadrome.

A boy learns who he is without magic: a young man who gives his gift away, and then must discover what remains of him.

The Story of the Trilogy

The Mirrorstone is no artifact: it is part of a network of water veins that the plateau people built and never maintained. Sayf al-Qadim, the last descendant of that people, wants the network under management again. On the opposite answer stands Tariq: the network must run free, without a caretaker. The trilogy begins with a boy who finds a stone, and ends with a man who lays one down.
