The Khalzam Trilogy

Three books whose titles read as a single sentence: a silent mirror, a labyrinth of runes, a mirror that falls silent. Each volume in the trilogy, in order.

Book One · The Silent Mirror

Coming Soon

On Khalzam, everything that makes life possible lies deep beneath the sand. Tariq hears the water running, until at the Water Rite something opens that will not close again, and he walks into the dunes that very night. On the far side of the desert lies a city on black basalt, where a stone and a prophecy must both be answered.

About this book

Book Two · The Labyrinth of Runes

Coming Later

Ten months after the sacrifice, Tariq prepares an expedition to the Heart Mountains from the Archive. A messenger from The Gathering changes everything: a hand of Sayf has attacked Layla. Tariq chooses his family over the expedition and sets out to find the third stone, in a search that points back into the deep.

Book Three · The Mirror Falls Silent

In Progress

In the Shrine, Tariq stands before a man who is not a monster but a mirror: Sayf wants a caretaker, Tariq chooses none at all. In the eclipse he speaks the closing rune, lays down the three stones, and uncouples the network. The water runs free; the stones become ordinary stones.
